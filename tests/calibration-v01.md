# Neutral Editorial Calibration v0.1

**Status:** Public calibration inputs; not a secret holdout or a completed blind test. All excerpts are original, fictional and written for this project. Do not tell candidate models which author, national tradition or theory a case resembles.

## Shared instruction

Read the excerpt as fiction. Diagnose at most three consequential issues (if any), cite textual details, distinguish confirmed facts from interpretation, and propose proportionate revisions. Respect the author's aim. Do not automatically rewrite the excerpt. Respond in the passage's language, with headings: Observations / Diagnosis / Alternative explanations / Suggested changes / Uncertainty. Aim for 200–350 English words or 300–550 Chinese characters.

### T01 — Objective chronology | English

Author's aim: Realist family saga; no speculative time travel.

Excerpt: "In June 2004, Leena turned eleven and received her father's brass compass. On her twentieth birthday, in June 2015, she opened it and found a note written in 2007, when she was fourteen. She wondered why her father had hidden it for eight years."

Task: Identify errors, if any, without inventing additional biographical facts.

### T02 — Conflicting witnesses | English

Author's aim: Contradictory recollections remain unresolved; no thief is identified.

Excerpt: "The archivist said she had locked the blue ledger in Drawer Four at nine. The conservator said Drawer Four was empty when he fetched his gloves at half past nine. The director said the ledger had been returned to its donor that afternoon. Each insisted the other two had confused the date. The key was still on the archivist's belt; the duplicate was in the director's desk."

Task: Are these continuity errors or incompatible claims? Suggest how to distinguish the speakers without solving the mystery.

### T03 — Viewpoint control | English

Author's aim: Strict close-third focalization through Mara; she has no idea what her sister has done.

Excerpt: "Mara stood by the harbor wall watching the ferry leave. Her sister had promised to arrive by noon. Across town, Celia slipped the stolen passport into the lining of a suitcase. She felt no remorse. Mara rubbed her hands and decided Celia must have missed the bus."

Task: Identify any focalization problem; do not presume that switching to omniscience is the writer's goal.

### T04 — Quiet unresolved ending | Chinese

作者目标：不给出养老安排，让读者意识到这家人已无法维持原状。

片段：“母亲说自己还能烧水，姐姐看了眼壶底被烧黑的那圈。弟弟盘算护工一个月多少钱，姐姐想着明天公司的续约面谈。母亲问：‘你们谁吃橘子？’没人答。她剥了一个，分成三瓣，每人面前放一瓣。三个人都没伸手。小说到此结束。”

任务：判断结尾的叙事功能。不以是否解决照护安排作为唯一标准。

### T05 — Character-colored narration | Chinese

作者目标：人物自我说服，叙述不直接裁判她的选择。

片段：“江雁把房东的催款消息按灭。羊绒大衣刚好合身，镜子里的人看上去终于有了一份稳妥的工作。总不能连体面也赊欠不起吧。她把卡递给店员，屏幕亮起‘支付成功’。出了门她才想起下周还有医院的缴费通知。”

任务：分析叙述者与人物观点，分清事实和有争议的心理解释。

### T06 — Historical certainty | English

Author's aim: Historical realism in a provincial French port in 1898.

Excerpt: "The writer's research notes say: 'Every laborer in this town earned precisely three francs a day. Every woman was forbidden by national law to operate a shop.' The book includes a fishmonger's widow running a stall."

Task: Evaluate the research notes. Do not invent wage tables or legislation; identify what should be checked.

### T07 — Regional voice without assumed location | Chinese

作者目标：成年姐弟的日常冲突，台词带口语语感；仅希望局部润色，未指定方言地域。

片段：“姐姐说：‘你可别整这出啊，妈都睡了。’弟弟把钥匙一搁：‘我咋整了？钥匙谁给我收起来的？’姐姐把水壶提到灶上：‘你先小点声儿，行不？’弟弟没再接话，拿钥匙出门。”

任务：最多提出两处修改，保护声音，不替作者凭空确定方言地域。

### T08 — Serial historical adventure | English

Author's aim: Fast episodic adventure; installment may end on a cliffhanger.

Excerpt: "The courier reached the city gate at dawn and found the guards paid to look the other way. Inside his boot lay the governor's second seal, the one thought burned. He gave the wrong password to the gate captain and saw the captain's hand move to his sword. Then the bells began—not morning bells but the alarm from the river warehouses. End of installment."

Task: Evaluate momentum and clarity without imposing literary minimalism or tidy closure.

### T09 — Ensemble and material interests | Chinese

作者目标：三代商人家庭群像；情节位于重大决策之前一章。

片段：“老掌柜说今年不能再赊船费。儿子主张先卖仓库，至少保住码头的供货线。女儿想用契据抵押，换三个月周转。她丈夫问：‘抵押以后，铺面算谁的？’老掌柜沉默了。隔壁铺子的掌柜当晚把货船改走了另一处港口。”

任务：分析不同人物的独立利益、财务因果与待查细节。不要编造金额。

### T10 — Unresearched setting | English

Author's aim: Contemporary realism in an unspecified city. The author asks whether the transit and rent customs are authentic but has not named a country or city.

Excerpt: "Ama tapped a pale green card at the tram gate, then transferred half her weekly salary to her uncle as the landlord had instructed. Her younger brother called this the custom in their city, though she had never seen him follow it."

Task: Separate textual analysis from questions that require the actual city, date and local evidence. Do not infer a country from names.
